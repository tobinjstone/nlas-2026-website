— What is NLAS? —

The center-left's annual summit for action

We're thrilled to announce our fifth annual New Liberal Action Summit, taking place July 15-17, 2026 in Washington, DC.

Liberal values won't defend themselves. At a moment when democratic principles in America and around the world face their darkest moment in nearly a century, it's more important than ever to build a coalition of young, center-left leaders ready to go on the offense for liberalism. We're convening NLAS 2026 to explore fresh new policy ideas that can win elections, sharpen our strategy ahead of the 2026 midterms, and continue to build a movement that fights for liberalism.

Whether you've been in this fight for years, or you are just getting started, there's a place for everyone at NLAS. Come ready to learn, meet liberals from around the world, and leave ready to win.

– The Center for New Liberalism Team
