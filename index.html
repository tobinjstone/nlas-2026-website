What is NLAS?

We're thrilled to announce the return of our New Liberal Action Summit, taking place July 15-17, 2026 in Washington, DC.

As Democrats look toward the midterms and beyond, NLAS 2026 will convene the next generation of center-left leaders to chart a winning path forward. Through policy workshops, professional training, and coalition-building, this year's summit will focus on rebuilding trust with voters, expanding our electoral appeal, and advancing liberal values.

Whether you're a longtime CNL member or joining us for the first time, this is your opportunity to be part of a movement to make liberalism win again.

– The Center for New Liberalism Team
