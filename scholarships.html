Scholarships & Financial Aid

We believe cost should never be a barrier to participation.

Available Support

  • Full Scholarships: Cover registration, travel, and lodging
  • Partial Scholarships: Cover registration fees
  • Travel Grants: Assistance with transportation costs

Who Should Apply?

Scholarships are available to students, young professionals, community organizers, and anyone for whom the cost of attendance would be a significant barrier. We especially encourage applications from underrepresented communities and first-time attendees.

How to Apply

Complete the scholarship application form below. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and you will be notified of your status via email. Please include a brief statement about why you'd like to attend NLAS 2026 and how it will support your work.
