Getting Around

DC's Metro system is your best friend during NLAS. Both Woolly Mammoth Theatre and Hilton Garden Inn are easily accessible via Metro:

Woolly Mammoth Gallery Place-Chinatown
Hilton Garden Inn Union Station or NoMa-Gallaudet U

We recommend getting a SmarTrip card for seamless travel throughout the weekend.

Getting to DC

DC is easy to reach by air or rail. Fly into one of two nearby airports, or if you're close enough, hop on Amtrak and ride straight into the heart of the city.

DCA Reagan National Closest to downtown, Metro connected
IAD Dulles International Main international hub, ~30 mi from downtown
🚆 DC Union Station Amtrak hub, steps from the hotel
