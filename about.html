— Our Organization —

About The Center for New Liberalism

A home for the pragmatic center-left

Twin Cities New Liberals Canvass for Mayor Jacob Frey

In an era defined by political polarization and gridlock, the Center for New Liberalism (CNL) represents a global network of activists, policy enthusiasts, and pragmatists who believe that liberal values are our most powerful tools for solving the defining challenges of the 21st century.

With over 60 chapters worldwide, we champion abundance, economic opportunity, and evidence-based solutions. As the 2026 midterm elections approach, our grassroots network is mobilizing to support a new generation of candidates who deliver results.
