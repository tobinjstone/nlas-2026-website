Frequently Asked Questions

General Information

The New Liberal Action Summit (NLAS) is the flagship annual conference organized by the Center for New Liberalism. It brings together center-left leaders, policy experts, activists, and community members to discuss ideas, develop skills, and build coalitions to advance liberal values.

NLAS 2026 will take place July 15-17, 2026 in Washington, DC. The exact venue will be announced soon.

NLAS is open to anyone passionate about advancing center-left policies and liberal values. Whether you're a seasoned policy professional, an elected official, a student, or an engaged citizen, you'll find valuable programming and networking opportunities at the summit.

The summit features keynote speeches from leading political figures and thinkers, policy workshops, professional training sessions, networking events, and coalition-building opportunities. You'll leave with new skills, connections, and ideas to make liberalism win.
